Weight unit

Each ring is a score out of 100 against a published benchmark, so a full ring is always good news. Vitals: Heart (resting heart rate, lower is better), Recovery (heart rate variability, higher is better) and Breathing (respiratory rate, best at 12 to 18). Fitness: Sleeping heart rate, Blood oxygen and VO₂ max (your percentile for age and sex). The number in the middle is the mean of the three.
